The problem
Customers
Need the right scientific capability quickly, without searching across fragmented channels.
Labs, CROs and Scientists
Need visibility into their capabilities and a steady pipeline of relevant R&D work, without constantly searching across fragmented channels.
Scimplify
Needs a scalable network connecting customer demand with scientific expertise.
Strategy
Build a demand-led R&D network.

Match what customers need with what labs, CROs and scientists can do, then make the path from opportunity to project simple.

Customer demand→Scinode→Labs, CROs and Scientists→Proposal→Project
Customers
Bring R&D requirements · discover capabilities · execute projects
Labs, CROs and Scientists
Discover opportunities · showcase expertise · collaborate · submit proposals · win projects
Scimplify
Match demand to expertise · make the path from opportunity to project simple
The product challenge
Create enough value for each side while keeping the researcher experience simple.
Product decisions
01
Product decision
Search → Surfaced demand
Demand before discovery
Instead of asking labs, CROs and scientists to search endlessly for work, we surfaced customer-generated R&D opportunities.
Decision  ·  An opportunity feed based on real demand.
02
Product decision
Generic profile → Capability-based profile
Expertise drives matching
Labs, CROs and scientists needed more than a generic profile.
Decision  ·  Structured researcher and CRO profiles built around capabilities, expertise and experience, so opportunities can be matched to what they actually do.
03
Product decision
Discovery → Delivery
Opportunity, proposal, project
Finding an opportunity isn’t the end goal.
Opportunity→Proposal→Negotiation→Project
Decision  ·  Connect discovery directly to proposal submission and project execution.
04
Product decision
Organic supply → Targeted acquisition
Build the network on purpose
Instead of waiting for organic supply, we actively built the supply side of the network through targeted outreach and institutional campaigns.
Decision  ·  Use targeted researcher acquisition to seed the marketplace.

What I learned
01
A marketplace isn’t activated by sign-up.
Onboarding and the first relevant opportunity were stronger signals of researcher activation.
02
Supply quality matters as much as supply volume.
More researchers only create value when their capabilities are structured well enough to match real demand.
03
The first opportunity is the critical moment.
The experience needs to get researchers from “I signed up” to “this project is relevant to me” quickly.
The next evolution is to make matching more intelligent: moving from opportunity discovery toward proactive demand-to-expertise matching.
