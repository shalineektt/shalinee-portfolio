Digii Campus

A two-sided campus registration product, with flows and acceptance criteria for both students and admins, plus a prototype.

Type
PRD, self-directed concept
Program
Product Space
Tools
Wireframes, prototype
The problem
Who it is for
Student
Registers for courses or exams and pays fees.
Admin
Sets up sessions and manages registrations.
What the PRD covers
→Problem, personas for both sides, and user stories.
→Scope, flows and wireframes for students and admins, with a prototype link.
→Technical and non-technical requirements, and edge cases.
→Acceptance criteria, a phased launch, and metrics for both user types.

If I revisited it
→Add evidence for the problem, such as time to register and failure rates, and baselines and targets for the metrics.
→Cover the biggest risk in a fee flow: payment succeeding while registration fails, double payment, eligibility checks and refunds.
→Add a non-goals list and a priority order.
→Test the design with a first-year student on a phone, not only a third-year.
→Reconcile the storage and payment choices between the requirements and the feature list.
