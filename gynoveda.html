The problem
Who it is for
Priya, the patient
The prioritised persona, with the reason and a prevalence figure stated.
Dr. Patel, the doctor
Too little time and too many patients.
What the PRD covers
→In-scope and out-of-scope lists, and non-technical requirements.
→A user flow with follow-up and a "mark as resolved" loop.
→A launch plan with dates: alpha, a Mumbai beta, marketing, then a Tier-I launch.
If I revisited it
→Protect the doctor’s time: triage by staff, office hours, reply templates, and a numeric response-time target.
→Add a safety net for urgent symptoms: an escalation path, a disclaimer and a way to catch red-flag messages.
→Base the compliance section on Indian rules. The PRD cited HIPAA, a US law.
