Context

Scinode Deep Research is an AI research workspace for chemists. It brings literature, patents, molecules and synthesis routes into one place, so a question can become a research path.

The problem

Researchers could find scientific information, but turning a question into a structured, evidence-backed research path was fragmented across multiple tools and sources.

Product goal

Make Deep Research the shortest path from a scientific question to an actionable research decision.

Success = Activation + Research depth + Repeat usage
The core experience
Question → research context → AI research. A molecule is resolved, then evaluated routes are scored and compared, with the best one flagged.

The shift
Researchers move from searching for information to building evidence-backed scientific decisions.
What success looks like
Success isn’t more searches. It’s helping researchers reach a confident next step with less research friction.
Key insights
01
Researchers don’t think in isolated searches. They work through evolving research contexts.
02
Research depth varies significantly by question, so a fixed unit of usage fits badly.
03
Continued research is a stronger signal of value than a single completed search.
Product decisions
01
Product decision
Search → Research context
Research context over isolated searches
Researchers often work across multiple questions and molecules, so we moved beyond isolated searches toward persistent research contexts.
A research session starts from a question, and can be tied to a live project.
02
Product decision
Molecule limits → Usage-based credits
Credits over molecule counts
Molecule-based limits didn’t map well to how researchers actually worked. We explored a research-consumption model using AI credits.
Where we started: fixed molecule limits.
Usage-based AI credits
Usage follows how deep the research goes.
Research is organised in projects, and AI credits are consumed in proportion to the work, instead of a fixed count of molecules.
Where we moved: usage-based credits.
03
Product decision
Private research → Controlled collaboration
Private by default, shared with control
Research was private by default, with team-based sharing introduced for collaboration and governance.

What I learned
