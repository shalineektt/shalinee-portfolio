If I revisited it
→Make the ranking consistent. A medium-impact item sat above high-impact ones, and a high-impact, low-effort feature was rated medium.
→Measure medication adherence, the outcome that matters, and set a north star.
→Address health-data privacy and say who the consulting doctors are.
→Raise the fidelity of the dashboard wireframe.
